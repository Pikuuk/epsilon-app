Who we are

Epsilon Academy ("we") provides online tuition for students in Years 7 to 13. The data controller is [your legal name/company], contactable at [your email/address].

What we collect and why

  • Account details: name and email (a parent or guardian email for students), year group and chosen subjects — to create and run the account.
  • Learning data: answers, practice results, progress and the questionnaire responses — to plan and improve each student's tuition.
  • Attendance: whether a student logged in for a scheduled session — to support learning and to contact parents about missed sessions.
  • Safeguarding: if something a student writes suggests they may be at risk, a flag is recorded for a named adult to act on.

Children and consent

For students under 13, a parent or guardian provides permission before the account is used. Parents can ask to see their child's data, correct it, or have it deleted.

Who can see the data

Only the student, their linked parent/guardian, the assigned teacher and the Epsilon administrator, each seeing only what their role needs. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.
